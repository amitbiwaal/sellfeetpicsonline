What is an affiliate link?

An affiliate link is a special tracking link. If you click it and then sign up or make a purchase on the partner's website, the partner may pay us a commission. The price you pay is exactly the same whether you use our link or go to the website directly. Sometimes our links even unlock a discount or bonus that is not available elsewhere, and we say so when that happens.

Which links are affiliate links?

Links to selling platforms, such as "Visit site" buttons on our Best Platforms page and some links inside our reviews and guides, may be affiliate links. We mark these links as sponsored in our code so search engines understand the relationship.

Our rankings are not for sale

  • We rank platforms using the same criteria for everyone: buyer traffic, ease of getting sales, seller fees, commission structure, payout reliability, privacy and verification, and long-term reputation.
  • We include and even recommend platforms that pay us nothing, and we point out the weaknesses of platforms that do pay us.
  • No platform can pay for a higher position, a better score or a positive review.
  • If a partnership ever conflicts with our readers' best interests, our readers win.

Why we are telling you this

We believe you deserve to know how this Site is funded. This disclosure is also made in line with the US Federal Trade Commission's guidance on endorsements and similar rules in other countries, which require publishers to clearly disclose material connections with the brands they mention.
