What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. They help websites work, remember your preferences and understand how visitors use them. Similar technologies include local storage and tracking pixels.

Cookies we use

Strictly necessary cookies

Regular readers do not need any cookies to use the Site. Our editors receive a secure login cookie when they sign in to the admin area, and a preview cookie when they preview unpublished articles. These cookies are essential for those features and are not used for tracking.

Analytics cookies (only if enabled)

If we switch on an analytics service such as Google Analytics, it may set cookies like _ga and _ga_* to count visits and see which pages are popular. These cookies can last up to two years. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent before they are used. You can opt out of Google Analytics at any time with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Third-party cookies from partner platforms

When you click a link to a selling platform, that platform may set its own cookies on its own website, for example to remember that you arrived from {{site_name}}. Those cookies are controlled by the platform and covered by its own cookie and privacy policies.

How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers also let you block third-party cookies only. Blocking cookies will not stop you from reading the Site. Helpful guides are available from the makers of each browser, including Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge.
